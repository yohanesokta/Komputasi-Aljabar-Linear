
```math
(-1)\ 0+1 \ \det(11) * M_{11}\\

```
```math
(+) a_{11} * M_{11} = (+) 0 * ((-2 * 1) - (-1 * 0))\\
```

## SOAL 3.

```math
A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & -3 & 1 & 1 \\ -3 & 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & -3 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & 1 & -3 \end{bmatrix}.
```
---
**Ekspansi Baris 1 matriks A (4x4):**
```math
(-1)\ 1+1 \ \det(11) * M_{11}\\
(-1)\ 1+2 \ \det(12) * M_{12}\\
(-1)\ 1+3 \ \det(13) * M_{13}\\
(-1)\ 1+4 \ \det(14) * M_{14}\\
```
```math
(+) a_{11} * M_{11} = (+) 1 * \det(M_{11})\\
(-) a_{12} * M_{12} = (-) -3 * \det(M_{12})\\
(+) a_{13} * M_{13} = (+) 1 * \det(M_{13})\\
(-) a_{14} * M_{14} = (-) 1 * \det(M_{14})\\
```
---
**Mencari nilai $\det(M_{11})$:**
```math
M_{11} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 & 1 \\ 1 & -3 & 1 \\ 1 & 1 & -3 \end{bmatrix}
```
---
```math
(-1)\ 1+1 \ \det(11) * M_{11}\\
(-1)\ 1+2 \ \det(12) * M_{12}\\
(-1)\ 1+3 \ \det(13) * M_{13}\\
```
```math
(+) a_{11} * M_{11} = (+) 1 * ((-3 * -3) - (1 * 1))\\
(-) a_{12} * M_{12} = (-) 1 * ((1 * -3) - (1 * 1))\\
(+) a_{13} * M_{13} = (+) 1 * ((1 * 1) - (-3 * 1))\\
```
---
```math
+ 1 * (9 - 1) \\
- 1 * (-3 - 1) \\
+ (1 * (1 - -3))\\
= 16
```
